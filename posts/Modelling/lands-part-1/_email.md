Dear reader,

It has been two years since my last post. Finishing my PhD and starting a postdoc kept me busier than expected, but the printing presses are running again, and with a topic rather different from our usual ones. This time we temporarily abandon pure mathematics and become wizards, duelling for magical supremacy (if you play MTG this will make sense, but don't worry, no knowledge of the game is assumed).

As every wizard knows, few things lose more duels than one's own lands, so in this post we ask the following questions:

1. How likely are we to draw the lands we need?
2. How does the calculation break down once a land can make more than one colour?
3. What do the best wizards do instead, and what are the limitations of their methods?
